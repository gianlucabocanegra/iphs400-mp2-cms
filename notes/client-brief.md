# Client brief: Golf & Country Club of Trujillo

**Who runs the site?** Probably the administrative office. I think they are comfortable with computers, but I have not confirmed who actually posts things.

**Who reads it, and what do they look for?** Members and their families. They mostly want the restaurant menu, upcoming events, and club news.

**What is broken now?** News and events go out on WhatsApp groups and Instagram. Messages get buried, they are hard to search, and there is no single place that is always up to date. Members end up asking the office the same questions again and again.

**What would make them say "this is better"?** One page where a member can find the current menu, the next events, and the latest news in a few seconds. And staff can post an update in a few minutes without asking anyone for help.

**What must NOT happen?**
- A staff member publishes an unfinished post or a wrong menu or price by mistake.
- Someone who is only supposed to post news changes the rules or membership pages, or adds or removes users.
- Anything still in draft shows up on the public site.
